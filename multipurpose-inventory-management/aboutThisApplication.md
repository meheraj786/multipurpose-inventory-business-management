# About This Application

## What this app is

This application is a business dashboard for managing inventory, purchases, sales, suppliers, customers, and stock.

It is meant to bring daily business operations together into a single view so the team can easily track:

- what is available in stock
- what is ordered from suppliers
- what has been sold
- how much is wasted or lost
- which customers and suppliers are active

## Who should use it

This app is intended for small and medium businesses that need a simple way to track:

- retail products
- restaurant raw ingredients and prepared goods
- service offerings
- supplier relationships
- customer sales and dues

## How people use it

Users typically work with the app by:

1. opening the dashboard to see overall performance
2. choosing a business area from the sidebar
3. recording purchases and supplier deliveries
4. recording sales and customer activity
5. checking stock levels and waste reports

## What it helps with

The app helps businesses:

- keep product and ingredient details in one place
- view sales performance at a glance
- monitor low stock and inventory alerts
- understand waste and loss across operations
- connect purchases with suppliers and customer sales

## Practical scope

The application is designed for the user experience rather than technical details. It focuses on:

- reporting and visibility
- smooth navigation between business areas
- simple sales and purchase tracking
- basic stock and waste monitoring

## What is not finished yet

- The staff and role permission module is still under development.
- The main inventory, sales, and purchase workflows are available.
- Access control is partially prepared, but not yet completed.

## Main sections

- Dashboard — overview of business health
- Products — product items and stock details
- Sales — sales records and due management
- Purchases — purchase orders and supplier relationships
- Suppliers — supplier profiles and history
- Customers — customer records and sales
- Raw products — ingredient and raw goods tracking
- Prepared products — prepared item inventory and production
- Wastage — monitoring lost or spoiled inventory
- Settings — app preferences and account details
- Activity log — recent business actions
- Trash — review deleted items later

## Why it exists

This app is meant to be a simple, practical tool for business owners and managers. It is not focused on technical implementation, but on helping teams keep their stock, orders, sales, and waste under control.
